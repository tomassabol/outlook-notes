Notes & Labels for Outlook

Private notes, B number, work order and labels for email conversations in new Outlook on Windows and Mac. Notes are stored on your computer; this site only serves the add-in's code.

Download manifest.xml

  1. Download manifest.xml with the button above.
  2. In new Outlook, open Apps (or Get add-ins) → My add-ins → Custom add-ins → Add a custom add-in → Add from file… and choose the file.
  3. Open an email and choose Notes & Labels in the message toolbar (it may be under Apps or the … menu).
  4. Click the pin icon at the top of the pane so it stays open as you move between emails.

Source and details: github.com/tomassabol/outlook-notes
